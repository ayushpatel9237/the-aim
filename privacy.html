Information we collect

  • Order information: name, delivery address, phone number, email address.
  • Payment information: processed directly by our payment gateway. We never see or store your full card or UPI credentials.
  • Usage information: basic analytics such as pages viewed, used to improve the store.

How we use it

Only to process and deliver your order, provide support, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay — to process your payment. Razorpay handles your card or UPI details directly; we never see or store them.
  • Supabase — our database provider, where your order is stored. Servers are located in Singapore.
  • Courier partners — your name, address and phone, so they can deliver your order.
  • Government authorities — only where we are legally required to disclose.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Cookies and local storage

We store your shopping bag in your browser's local storage so it survives a refresh. This stays on your device.

How we protect it

Your order data is held in a database with row-level security, so it can only be read by you and by us — not by other customers and not by anonymous visitors. Payments are verified on our server, not in your browser. The site is served over HTTPS.

Data retention

Order records — including your name, address and phone number — are kept for 8 years, the period Indian tax law requires us to retain business records. After that they are deleted.

If you have an account with us, your saved details are kept until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong or out of date
  • delete your information, where we are not required by law to keep it
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights if you are unable to

Email theaim.9237@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. There is no charge.
