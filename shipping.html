Shipping Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Where we ship

We currently ship across India. We do not ship internationally.

Dispatch time

Orders are dispatched within 2 working days of payment confirmation. Orders placed on Sundays and public holidays are dispatched the next working day.

Delivery time

Once dispatched, delivery typically takes 3–7 working days depending on your location. Metro cities are usually at the shorter end; remote pincodes may take longer. Delivery time is handled by our courier partner and is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Delivery charges

Delivery is free on all orders across India. There is no minimum order value and no hidden charge at checkout — the price you see on the product page is the price you pay.

Tracking

Once your order is dispatched we send tracking details by email and WhatsApp. You can also track any order yourself at any time on our Track Order page using your order number and the phone number you ordered with.

Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery is available across India at no extra charge. Please keep the exact amount ready — couriers may not carry change.

Failed delivery

Couriers normally attempt delivery up to 3 times. If all attempts fail because the address was incomplete or nobody was available, the parcel returns to us. We will contact you to arrange redelivery. If you would prefer a refund instead, we will refund the full amount you paid.
